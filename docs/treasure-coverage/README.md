## Denominator and limitations

One entry = one exact power ID + tier ID + effect index. Different published tiers count separately, even when their sentences repeat. The primary denominator is **21808 outgoing damage-relevant entries with a resolved official combat-skill association**. Generic/AnySkill, Unknown and noncombat associations are inventoried separately, never duplicated into all skill rows. Zero denominators display —. Official Combat=true includes Angling, Riding and Performance skills; these are not silently omitted.

Relevance and family classification use explicit structured IDs/labels and conservative text screening for audit only. This is **not an exhaustive human interpretation of every sentence** or a formula parser. Ambiguous damage prose stays in the denominator; non-damage resources, taunt, reuse and incoming defense are excluded. Effects with unresolved attribute meaning are listed separately (0). Cooldown/resource-only changes may affect future DPS but are outside per-use damage. Inspect source sentences to refine classification; no runtime formula trusts this screening.

Fully modeled means the effect's outgoing contribution reaches applicable published combat hits with no known omitted clause/delivery/allocation. Partially modeled means a direct contribution works but another clause or delivery/allocation remains unsupported. **Coverage = fully modeled / damage-relevant**, with no partial-credit weighting. Counts do not mean observed game-formula correctness. Relevant Mods visibility is never used.

Before uses the T0014 direct-hook registry and its two Mock rules, by disabling keyword rules and all new curated rules. After probes the same pinned inputs through the actual aggregator and damage engine. Baseline **4914 full + 176 partial (22.53% full; 23.34% with any modeled contribution)**; after **4979 full + 1029 partial (22.83% full; 27.55% with any modeled contribution)**. **918 previously unmodeled combat entries across 40 powers gain support.** Stacking/order remains assumed; no new in-game measurements were made.

## Reproduction and follow-ups

`npm ci && npm run coverage:treasure && npm run coverage:treasure -- --check` regenerates deterministic artifacts and verifies byte equality. Decompress with `gzip -dc docs/treasure-coverage/effects.json.gz`. No network or changing live data is used.

Follow-ups before claiming complete damage coverage: (1) curate remaining stateless prose in small source-reviewed batches; (2) measure multi-component flat allocation and delivery-specific stacking; (3) research conditional/triggered/pet/DoT/crit mechanics in separately scoped tickets; (4) collect current Burst measurements to replace interpolation and resolve radius remainders. These are not implemented as T0015 Sparks.
